Active Recall — The Only Technique That Works

💡 Re-reading = weight dekhna. Active Recall = weight uthana. Brain ko struggle chahiye memory ke liye.
1
Padho — actively, ek baar
Topic padho. Underline mat, highlight mat. Sirf mentally process karo.
2
Band karo — dump karo
Kitaab band. Kagaz lo. Jo bhi yaad hai sab likho bina dekhe. Galat ho, incomplete ho — no problem.
3
Check karo — sirf gaps padho
Kitaab kholo. Kya chhoota? SIRF wahi dobara padho. Poora topic nahi.
4
Agle din — 10 min recall
Padhai se pehle kal ka topic bina dekhe likho. Yehi Spaced Repetition hai — long-term memory isi se banti hai.
